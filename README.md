## 👋 About us

StackNoor is a web development studio that designs and builds software for businesses worldwide. We turn ideas and manual processes into clean, fast, reliable products, from a business website to a full custom platform with an AI assistant built in.

## 🛠️ What we build

- **Custom web applications**: dashboards, portals, booking systems, SaaS platforms
- **AI chatbots & automation**: assistants that answer customers, capture leads and handle repetitive work 24/7
- **Business websites**: fast, SEO-ready sites that turn visitors into clients
- **E-commerce**: online stores with payments, inventory and order management
- **APIs & integrations**: connecting your tools, payment gateways and third-party services
- **Speed & SEO optimization**: faster pages, better rankings, higher conversions
